About

The position at the top of Indian digital marketing is still open.

GraphikosX is being built to take it — by going deep into ten industries instead of wide into a hundred.

India has thousands of digital marketing agencies. But no one is truly on the top. That position is still open. GraphikosX is being built to take that position.

Most agencies promise everything to every business. They build dashboards, sell packages, and become average at most things. I started GraphikosX with a different conviction: that specialisation, depth, and personal accountability are how you build real reputation.

I don't believe in generic marketing.

I believe every business deserves a strategy that understands its industry, customers, and trust barriers. A doctor's marketing should not look like a real-estate developer's marketing. A jeweller's strategy should not be the same playbook as a CA firm's. Each industry has its own customer psychology, buying journey, and trust signals — and the agency serving them should treat each with that depth.

That is why GraphikosX serves only ten industries. Each one studied closely. Each one served with care.

Why ten industries.

Doctors and clinics. Gyms and fitness studios. Cafes and restaurants. Real estate and builders. Architecture and interior designers. The chemical industry. Education and coaching institutes. Automobile and EV dealerships. Jewellery and wedding businesses. Legal services and CA firms.

These ten represent industries where customer trust is built primarily online today, where the difference between an average digital presence and a great one directly translates into revenue, and where most operators are still under-serving the digital opportunity.

How I work.

I personally oversee every account. There is no agency hierarchy hiding behind dashboards. There is no team of juniors guessing at strategy while the founder is unreachable. When you work with GraphikosX, you are working with me — supported by a small team of specialists I've personally trained.

This is intentional. It limits how many clients I can take at once. It also ensures that every brief, every reel, every campaign is informed by someone who actually understands your industry and is personally accountable for the outcome.

What I want to build.

The most respected specialist digital agency in India. One that serves ten industries with depth no generalist can match. One that operators in those industries trust as their long-term digital partner — not their next short-term experiment.

— Prakash Pal
